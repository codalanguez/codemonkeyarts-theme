CodeMonkeyArts Signal Lab

A home for apps, workflows, and experiments.

Signal Lab brings the theatrical character of Coda Languez’s author site into a creative-engineering portfolio. Antique-gold headings and buttons sit against deep ink, with cyan circuit grids and magenta chromatic accents. The terminal illustration is built in CSS, and the text stays readable through every effect.

inside the theme

Built for creative engineering.

Gold, circuitry, and a little interference.
Forum display lettering and Cormorant Garamond body copy keep the literary connection. A terminal panel, circuit grids, scanlines, and short wordmark glitches give the Lab its own identity.
Projects take the stage.
A dedicated homepage introduces the work, while responsive cards and app pages provide room for previews, descriptions, and clear next steps.
Motion on your terms.
An Effects toggle remembers your preference in the browser. System reduced-motion settings take priority, and decorative animation never scrambles the readable text.
A focused social bar.
GitHub, Payhip, Civitai, Substack, and Threads connect the Lab to the places where the work continues.
Classic WordPress, carefully assembled.
PHP templates, lightweight CSS, a small motion-control script, and locally hosted fonts. Built for WordPress 6.0 or later and PHP 7.4 or later. Theme code is GPL-2.0-or-later; bundled fonts retain their own licenses.

start building

Make it your own.

  1. Download the Signal Lab theme ZIP above.
  2. In WordPress, go to Appearance → Themes → Add Theme → Upload Theme and upload the ZIP.
  3. Preview and activate CodeMonkeyArts Signal Lab, then set your homepage under Settings → Reading.

The ZIP includes the theme; demo pages and project content are not imported. The public repository includes setup notes and editable page examples, plus the source for customizing the branding and social links.
