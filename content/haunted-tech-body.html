Haunted Tech

Literary atmosphere. A little interference.

The full-site-editing theme running Coda Languez’s author site. Gold lettering and oxblood accents meet deep black, art-deco frames, CRT scanlines, and glitch effects. Story carousels, a 3D-spine bookshelf, and dedicated reading layouts give fiction and artwork a theatrical home. MIT licensed; haunt responsibly.

inside the theme

A stage for strange stories.

Gold, shadow, and static.
Literary typography, art-deco framing, gold highlights, and oxblood details carry the author identity. CRT scanlines and chromatic glitches add a little interference.
Stories lead the way.
A story carousel, serial reader paths, book shelves, and dedicated book, web novel, and chapter layouts help readers find their next obsession.
Build with WordPress blocks.
Full Site Editing templates and dynamic blocks let you arrange the homepage, header, footer, gallery, and reading sections in the Site Editor.
Your identity, your connections.
Set your logo, primary and social menus, newsletter provider, and story content. ACF supplies the structured fields used by the book and serial features.
Readable through the effects.
Self-hosted fonts, responsive layouts, keyboard carousel controls, and reduced-motion support keep the presentation usable.

start haunting

Make it your own.

  1. Download the Haunted Tech theme ZIP above. Install ACF 6.x for the theme’s structured content fields.
  2. In WordPress, go to Appearance → Themes → Add Theme → Upload Theme and upload the ZIP.
  3. Preview and activate Haunted Tech. Add your own logo, menus, newsletter connection, books, and serial content using the repository’s setup guide.

The download contains the theme, assets, and setup README. Demo stories, artwork collections, and site-specific integrations are not imported. The screenshot shows the configured author site; a fresh installation needs your content and settings.
